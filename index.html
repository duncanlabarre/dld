The Abbey DLD Group of Colleges

Recognised around the world for providing high-quality education

The Abbey DLD Group of Colleges consists of three independent day and boarding school located in Cambridge, London and Manchester. We offer a range of academic programmes for students from 13 – 21 years. Since 1931 our group has been recognised around the world for providing high-quality education, enabling our students to progress to top-ranked university destinations, and for providing enriching student experiences.
